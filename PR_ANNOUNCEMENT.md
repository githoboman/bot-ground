# Stackpad is Live

We are thrilled to announce that Stackpad has **Officially launched on BOT Chain Mainnet**!

## Why BOT Chain?
BOT Chain provides the ideal high-performance, low-cost environment for our Pay-As-You-Read eBook platform. With seamless EVM compatibility and the robust BOT token economy, authors can instantly receive micro-payments as readers consume their content page-by-page.

## What's Next?
Readers can now connect their EVM wallets directly to our platform. Simply add the BOT Chain Mainnet to your wallet, bridge or acquire some BOT tokens, and start discovering new books without the upfront cost.

*Read by the chapter. Pay as you go. No subscriptions, no waste.*
